# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The portfolio is for engineering hiring managers, technical collaborators, independent-product peers, photography clients, and readers who want a fast but honest picture of Fengyi Zhang. Most arrive on desktop from a resume, GitHub profile, or shared link. They need to understand the professional arc quickly, then discover the personal work without the site feeling like two unrelated identities.

## Product Purpose

Present Fengyi as one person with several serious practices: a software engineer building large-scale spatial and AI systems, an independent product maker, a photographer, a writer, and a home-recording musician. Success means a visitor can verify concrete engineering outcomes in under two minutes, while the deeper archive gives them reasons to stay, remember the work, and make contact.

## Brand Personality

Archival, lucid, personal. The site should feel like an edited personal annual, an exhibition catalogue, and a well-kept engineering notebook sharing one binding.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer portfolios with terminal cosplay, dark navy dashboards, glowing gradients, floating glass cards, skill-logo clouds, inflated metrics, or a centered hero followed by identical project tiles. Avoid nostalgia that becomes costume: distressed paper textures, fake typewriter fonts, sepia filters, ornamental stamps on every surface, and editorial styling that hides the actual work.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a real person and a clear present-tense practice.
- Let evidence carry authority: shipped products, measured outcomes, real screenshots, photographs, writing, and playable sound.
- Treat career and personal practice as chapters in one life, not separate brands.
- Build an expandable archive, so future projects and annual photography or writing collections have an obvious home.
- Keep bilingual content first-class and navigation usable by keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion users.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets where practical, meaningful image descriptions, and a reduced-motion mode. English and Chinese versions should expose the same information hierarchy without layout breakage.
